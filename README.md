## The entry

> Weight limit reached. The buffet was your idea, not mine.

A 57-character one-liner, one line, no trailing newline. The elevator blames
the passenger's buffet choices for hitting its weight limit — a weight-limit /
buffet angle with zero overlap against the 15 public entries scanned
(thank-you, pressed-all-buttons, stuck-between-floors, and the rest).

## The contest

- One line, 1–120 UTF-8 characters.
- Winner takes all, 1st place only.
- 3 blind rounds; the final round is scored on the latest entry.
- `main.py` writes the entry byte-for-byte to
  `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (UTF-8, binary write, no trailing newline)
  and `verify()` reads it back to confirm bytes, length, and no newline.
